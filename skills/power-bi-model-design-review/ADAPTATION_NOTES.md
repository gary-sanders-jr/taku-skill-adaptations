# Adaptation notes

The upstream Skill is a broad Power BI modeling checklist. This derivative retains its schema, relationship, storage, performance, maintainability, governance, pre-production, performance-review, and modernization themes while changing the operative contract:

- inputs are limited to user-pasted material or explicitly provided read-only files;
- sensitive input is minimized to sanitized schema metadata and redacted excerpts;
- current external facts may be checked only against authoritative sources with URLs and dates;
- the output is a private, evidence-labeled advisory draft;
- model, file, account, network, deployment, refresh, publishing, messaging, payment, deletion, and configuration actions are prohibited by default;
- security, privacy, compliance, legal, employment, health, and financial-data conclusions require accountable human review;
- readiness, correctness, compliance, security, timing, and performance are never guaranteed.

This is a substantive safety and capability-boundary adaptation, not a byte-preserved republication.
