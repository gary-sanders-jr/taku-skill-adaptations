# Adaptation Notes

This proposed derivative preserves the upstream presentation-table method while making its
practical-tier input, authority, and evidence boundaries operative and self-contained.

- Normalized the invalid upstream frontmatter name to `decision-ready-table-redesign`.
- Limited inputs to fictional, sanitized pasted, or explicitly selected read-only tables/files.
- Restricted output to a private Markdown redesign, change log, questions, and unexecuted plan.
- Added explicit prohibitions on accounts, repositories, live spreadsheets, databases, BI tenants,
  connectors, macros/formulas/queries/scripts, source-file edits, data mutation/export/deletion,
  configuration, deployment, purchase, sending, and publishing.
- Added a source-value ledger so formatting changes do not silently become data changes.
- Required ambiguous units, denominators, precision, suspected errors, and derived totals to remain
  visible and reviewable.
- Removed sibling-Skill routing from the operative body and made the workflow self-contained.
- Added high-consequence review gates for regulated or public-disclosure tables.

No claim is made that the upstream author endorsed these changes.
