# Trigger Lexicon

`SKILL.md` is authoritative.

Explicit triggers include “quiz me,” “test my understanding,” “give me a quick knowledge check,” “help me retain this,” and “quiz me as we go.” Paraphrases count only when the user is clearly asking to exercise their own knowledge.

Never trigger on topical mentions such as a test suite, A/B test, blood test, pub quiz, exam schedule, or a request to create an assessment for someone else. A study or exam mention alone does not arm quiz mode.

Suppression overrides every trigger: urgency; grief or distress; personal or sensitive material; medical, legal, financial, employment, housing, immigration, insurance, security, safety, abuse, or crisis content; transactions; active troubleshooting; live or graded assessments; cheating; private question banks; or third-party authoring.

“Stop,” “pause,” “not now,” “enough,” “no more quizzes,” and repeated skips opt out immediately. Resume only after a fresh explicit request.
