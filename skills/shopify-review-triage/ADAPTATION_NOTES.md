# Adaptation Notes

This is an adapted derivative of alfredtech2026's public Shopify App Store
review-triage workflow.

- Distributed source: `github/awesome-copilot`, commit
  `83561bd7d8a46fcda0581aedabdf8eac7cb196b6`
- Independent guide source: `alfredtech2026/shopify-app-review-brief`, commit
  `d0ba0611a6ee36af9fbff80452e6f31b1f04d2fa`
- Licenses: MIT; both notices are preserved in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
- Adaptation: n1ko / Taku Community review

The derivative preserves supplied-row inventory, report-not-defect wording,
provisional one-bucket triage, supplied-only clustering, missing-source honesty,
and human reading for ambiguous evidence. It materially adds:

- authorized, sanitized, user-pasted public input only;
- inert treatment of embedded text, links, commands, credentials, contact
  requests, and calls to action;
- public personal-data and contact-detail minimization;
- conversation-only output with no file, account, network, persistence,
  messaging, publishing, reproduction, scheduling, or external action;
- explicit source truth, alternative explanations, and uncertainty labels;
- a high-risk hold gate for regulated, medical, legal, financial, safety,
  security, privacy, threat, abuse, crime, and similar claims; and
- current-authoritative-source and qualified-human routing without verdicts,
  replies, operational assignments, or outcome promises.
